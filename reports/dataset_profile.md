# Dataset profile

Generated by `python scripts/profile_data.py --config configs/base.yaml [--include-benchmark]`. Machine-readable outputs: `results/dataset_profile.json`, `results/dataset_profile_tracks.csv` (per-track shape statistics, IDs only), and `results/dataset_profile_table.md`.

## Cover Analysis selection (development data)

Selection: lexicographically sorted WIDs → `default_rng(20260817)` permutation → skip invalid → fill roles (D-008). **0 WIDs were excluded**: every one of the 1,720 walked WIDs had exactly two PIDs, readable H5 files, matching `label`/`track_id` attributes, finite values, non-zero energy and at least 96 frames.

| coveranalysis / query | 20 | 40 | 10,418 | 18,401 | 34,815 | 0 | 0.66 |

Across the 3,440 selected tracks, raw HPCP length has min 10,325, 5th percentile 11,686, median 18,197, mean 19,112, 95th percentile 30,530 and max 41,294 frames. The Cover Analysis metadata lists 5,000 WIDs, all with exactly two PIDs; the remaining 3,280 WIDs are not assigned in this run (D-010).

"Silent frames" are frames whose 12 HPCP bins are all zero. They stay zero after normalisation and cost 1 against every frame.

## Feature format (verified)

* Stored as `hpcp` float32 of shape `(T, 12)`, max-normalised per frame (values in [0, 1]). `label` and `track_id` are H5 attributes and match the manifest for every loaded file.
* Internally everything is `(12, T)` (`features/hpcp.py::to_pitch_by_time`). Orientation is inferred from the unique axis of size 12, and a `(12, 12)` array is refused as ambiguous rather than guessed.
* Classical view: frame-L2 → exact area-average resampling to 96 frames → frame-L2. With a median T of about 18,200, each classical frame averages about 190 raw frames. Encoder cache: the same recipe at 512 frames (about 36 raw frames per cached frame), pooled to the encoder input length.

Figure: `figures/profile_raw_vs_resampled.png` (raw vs. 96-frame HPCP for the first calibration work).
